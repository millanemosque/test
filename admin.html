Mill Lane · Timetable admin

1. Start a month

Pick the month from the new WhatsApp timetable. This creates Friday, Saturday and Sunday rows with the usual times filled in — you then just correct Asr and Isha.

Shortcut: paste text from the photo

On iPhone, open the WhatsApp photo, press and hold the text to select it (Live Text) and Copy. On Android use Google Lens → “Copy text”. Paste it here and tap Read. Always check the result below.

2. Check the times

Changing Friday's Asr or Isha copies it to that Saturday and Sunday. Write “After Sunset” for Maghrib to show the live sunset time. Leave a box empty if there is no jama'at.
